The '''Landsailer''' is a mount that can be obtained as a reward from [[The Great Expedition]] world quest.

== Obtaining ==
Players can purchase this mount from [[Proprietor Piers]] south of [[Fibula]] during the reward phase (August 12–18) for '''140 Event Points''', provided their server successfully completed the preparation phase.
